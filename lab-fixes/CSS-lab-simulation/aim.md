# Aim

To study the mathematical foundation of the **RSA Public-Key Cryptosystem**, evaluate its security reliance on the computational intractability of the **Prime Factorization Problem** for large semiprime moduli $n = p \times q$, and demonstrate how textbook (unpadded) RSA is fundamentally vulnerable to a **Chosen Ciphertext Attack (CCA)** through modular multiplicative homomorphic properties.
